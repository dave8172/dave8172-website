## Hi, I'm <RandomColor>Dave</RandomColor>

I'm an <RandomColor>AI reliability engineer.</RandomColor>

My focus is on the gap between "it works in the demo" and "I'd stake my name on this in production." That gap is where most AI systems live, and closing it — build it, validate it, audit it, whatever it takes — is the work I find most interesting.

---

## What I do

- **Build the reliability layer** — labeled eval sets, accuracy and failure reports, confidence scoring, audit logs, PII redaction. Retrofitted onto an existing pipeline, or built in from day one.
- **Validate and benchmark models** — does GPT-4o or a specialized OCR API fit your use case? I'll answer that with numbers, not opinions.
- **Audit AI systems already in production** — an independent check, with a report you can hand to your own team, a client, or an auditor.
- **Secure, self-hostable kits** — for environments where sensitive data can't leave the building.

The common thread: I care about what happens after the demo. Reliability, auditability, and security are the defaults — not the add-ons you bolt on when something breaks. My deepest proof of this so far is in document and financial AI extraction, but the same rigor applies wherever an AI system touches <RandomColor>data you can't afford to get wrong.</RandomColor>
